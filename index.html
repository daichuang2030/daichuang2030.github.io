A little about me

At the intersection of ideas and industry.

I’m Peter DAI Chuang. My work sits where enterprise leadership, digital systems, and artificial intelligence meet. I’m interested in what happens after the demonstration: the redesign of work, the growth of a team, and the moment a promising technology becomes genuinely useful.

Today, I serve as a Vice President of Chery Automobile and lead Chery’s global innovation business in Hong Kong. I bring an industry practitioner’s perspective to a larger question: how can intelligent systems expand what people and organizations are capable of?
